Absence Management in Oracle Fusion HCM handles leave policies, accrual calculations, and absence tracking for your organization.

## Absence Architecture

Key components of absence management:
- Leave types: Paid time off, sick leave, etc.
- Accrual policies: How leave is earned
- Carryover rules: Unused leave handling
- Absence requests: Employee leave submission

## Leave Types

Define leave categories for your organization:

### Statutory Leave
- Vacation: Paid time off
- Sick Leave: Illness-related absence
- Bereavement: Death-related leave
- Parental: Birth or adoption leave

### Administrative Leave
- Training: Professional development
- Jury Duty: Legal obligation
- Military Service: Military reserve
- Unpaid Leave: Without compensation

## Accrual Policies

Define how leave is earned:

### Accrual Methods
- Service-based: Increases with years
- Fixed: Same amount for all
- Fixed Anniversary: Resets each anniversary
- Scheduled: Varies by date

### Accrual Calculation
Accruals run automatically:
- Monthly: Accrue each month
- Quarterly: Accrue each quarter
- Annually: Accrue each anniversary
- As-needed: Manual accrual

## Carryover Rules

Manage unused leave:

### Carryover Policies
- No carryover: Lose unused balance
- Limited carryover: Maximum carry forward
- Full carryover: Carry all unused
- Decay: Reduce carryover over time

## Request Management

Employee leave request workflow:

### Request Process
1. **Submission**: Employee submits request
2. **Validation**: System checks availability
3. **Approval**: Manager reviews and approves
4. **Deduction**: Balance automatically adjusted
5. **Payroll**: Marked in payroll system

### Approval Rules
- Manager approval required
- Multiple approval levels
- Auto-approval for eligible requests
- Exception handling for edge cases

## Entitlements

Manage employee leave entitlements:

### Entitlement Components
- Base entitlement: Standard allocation
- Adjustments: Special grants or corrections
- Carryover: From previous period
- Current balance: Available for use

## Absence Reporting

Track and report on absence:

### Reports Generated
- Absence Summary: Total by employee
- Leave Balance: Current available
- Absence Trends: Historical patterns
- Audit Trail: Change history

## Compliance Considerations

Ensure regulatory compliance:
- Statutory minimums: Meet legal requirements
- Portability: Correct state regulations
- Disclosure: Proper accounting treatment
- Records: Maintain audit trail

## Integration with Payroll

Absence data affects payroll:
- Hours adjustment: Time not worked
- Earnings calculation: Leave paid as earnings
- Deduction: Balance reduction
- Accrual: Ongoing tracking

## Multi-Country Considerations

Managing global leave requirements:
- Country-specific rules
- Local statutory minimums
- Currency and payment treatment
- Reporting requirements

## Best Practices

1. **Establish clear policies** documented for employees
2. **Configure policies** matching legal requirements
3. **Test accruals** thoroughly before go-live
4. **Communicate changes** to affected employees
5. **Monitor usage** for patterns and issues

## Conclusion

Proper absence management ensures fair treatment, legal compliance, and accurate payroll processing. Invest in thorough policy definition and system configuration.

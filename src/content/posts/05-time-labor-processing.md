Time and Labor functionality in Oracle Fusion HCM manages employee work hours, attendance, and timekeeping requirements across the organization.

## Time and Labor Architecture

The Time and Labor module integrates:
- Time entry and tracking
- Attendance management
- Leave administration
- Payroll integration

## Timesheet Configuration

Establish timesheet structure for your organization:

### Timesheet Period
- Duration: Weekly, bi-weekly, monthly
- Start and end dates
- Approval deadlines
- Payment period alignment

### Work Schedule
Define expected work patterns:
- Full-time schedules
- Part-time arrangements
- Shift-based work
- Flexible schedules

## Recording Time Entries

Employees submit time through various methods:
- Mobile applications
- Web-based timesheets
- Time clocks
- Integration with badge systems

### Entry Types
- Regular Hours: Standard work time
- Overtime: Time exceeding normal hours
- Leave: Absence periods
- Adjustments: Corrections to prior entries

## Attendance Management

Track and manage employee presence:

### Attendance Policies
- Punctuality requirements
- Absence reporting procedures
- Documentation requirements
- Disciplinary actions

### Absence Types
- Paid Time Off
- Unpaid Leave
- Sick Leave
- Bereavement
- Maternity/Paternity

## Leave Administration

Manage leave entitlements and accruals:

### Leave Setup
- Accrual rules: How leave is earned
- Carryover: Unused leave handling
- Usage restrictions: When leave applies
- Documentation: Supporting records

### Leave Request Process
1. Employee requests leave
2. Manager approval
3. Leave deduction from balance
4. Payroll notification

## Approval Workflows

Establish multi-level approval structures:
- Manager review and approval
- Department head sign-off
- Compliance validation
- Final authorization

## Payroll Integration

Time and labor data flows to payroll:
- Hours worked calculations
- Overtime identification
- Leave processing
- Earnings determination

## Reporting Capabilities

Time and Labor generates reports for:
- Timesheet Summary: Hours by employee
- Attendance Report: Presence tracking
- Leave Usage: Accrual and consumption
- Payroll Input: Hour submission to payroll

## Compliance Considerations

Ensure compliance with labor regulations:
- Wage and hour laws
- Overtime regulations
- Leave requirements
- Accessibility requirements

## Best Practices

1. **Establish clear policies** communicated to all employees
2. **Configure approval workflows** matching organization
3. **Integrate with payroll** for accurate processing
4. **Monitor exceptions** for policy violations
5. **Maintain audit trails** for compliance

## Conclusion

Proper Time and Labor configuration ensures accurate hour tracking, compliance with regulations, and seamless payroll processing. Invest in clear policies and thorough implementation for optimal results.

Compensation Management in Oracle Fusion HCM enables strategic planning, equitable salary management, and performance-based rewards.

## Compensation Framework

The compensation framework establishes:
- Salary structures
- Grade scales
- Market positioning
- Performance linkage

## Salary Structures

Define hierarchical compensation frameworks:

### Structure Components
- Grade: Seniority level
- Midpoint: Target salary for grade
- Minimum: Entry salary for grade
- Maximum: Maximum salary for grade

### Salary Grades
Grades categorize positions by responsibility:
- Individual Contributor: Non-management roles
- Supervisor: Team leadership
- Manager: Department management
- Executive: Senior leadership

## Market Positioning

Align internal pay with external markets:
- Market Data: Industry compensation surveys
- Positioning: Where you pay relative to market
- Competitiveness: Ability to attract talent
- Retention: Ability to retain employees

## Salary Planning Process

Annual or periodic salary planning:

### Planning Steps
1. **Analysis**: Review current compensation
2. **Planning**: Establish merit budgets
3. **Review**: Evaluate individual performance
4. **Adjustment**: Propose salary changes
5. **Approval**: Management authorization
6. **Implementation**: Apply changes

## Performance-Based Compensation

Link compensation to performance:

### Performance Metrics
- Goals Achievement: Individual goal attainment
- Competency Demonstration: Skills and abilities
- Behavioral Assessment: Cultural fit
- Business Impact: Contribution to results

### Variable Compensation
- Bonuses: One-time performance payments
- Commissions: Sales-based compensation
- Incentives: Program-based rewards

## Benefits Administration

Manage employee benefits offerings:

### Benefits Components
- Health Insurance
- Retirement Plans
- Life Insurance
- Wellness Programs

## Equity and Pay Fairness

Ensure equitable compensation:
- Equal Pay Analysis: Gender/demographic pay gaps
- Variance Reports: Deviation from structures
- Compa-Ratio: Actual vs target pay
- Distribution Analysis: Pay spread by grade

## Compensation Reporting

Generate insights through reports:
- Salary Summary: Compensation by department
- Merit Increase Report: Proposed changes
- Compa-Ratio Analysis: Internal equity assessment
- Market Comparison: vs. industry benchmarks

## Compliance

Maintain compensation compliance:
- Equal Employment Opportunity
- Fair Labor Standards Act
- State Wage Laws
- Executive Compensation Disclosure

## Best Practices

1. **Establish clear compensation philosophy**
2. **Communicate compensation strategy** to employees
3. **Link to performance** and organizational values
4. **Review equity regularly** for fairness
5. **Monitor market changes** for competitiveness

## Conclusion

Strategic compensation management attracts, motivates, and retains top talent while maintaining organizational equity and legal compliance.
